# Why Aquis & What You Know About Them

## Who they are

Europe’s challenger exchange, now part of SIX (Swiss & Spanish financial infrastructure).

## 4 Divisions

- Aquis Markets: Pan-European equities trading (lit & dark books).
- Aquis Technologies: Matching engines and trade surveillance software.
- Aquis Stock Exchange (AQSE): Primary listings for growth companies (Access & Apex markets).
- Aquis Data: Market data sales.

## Key Differentiator

Subscription-based pricing charged on message traffic rather than trade value percentage.

## The Pitch

"Because Aquis operates on a message-traffic subscription model, high platform availability, low latency, and smooth technical operations are core to the business. This role's blend of platform triage, L1/L2 client support, and cross-departmental coordination matches my exact background."
