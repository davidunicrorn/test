# Questions to ask

Ask these with the recruiter and again with Jack. They show you are trying to understand the desk and where you would help.

1. Which platforms does this seat cover first — Aquis Markets, client exchanges, AQSE — or the whole set?
2. Where does Exchange Operations spend most of its time right now: live client issues, or standing up the next venue?
3. When a connected client is down, who takes the first call, and when do Surveillance, Infrastructure, or engineering get pulled in?
4. How much of the day is session health and message traffic, versus trade breaks and missing execution reports?
5. When a new client or venue comes on, is that mostly FIX session setup, or a heavier platform build-out?
6. What does a strong first 90 days look like on this desk?
7. Which of Linux, SQL, and FIX logs is the daily tool, and where do Grafana or binary protocols actually show up?
8. After a production outage, how do the outage report and the next release review change what the team does?

## Hold back

Rota, London days, salary band (£85,000–£95,000), start date, and right to work. A serious process will clarify those before an offer.
