# Questions to ask

## Ask the recruiter (first round)

1. Who is the hiring manager, and is the next round with the Head of Second Line Operations?
2. Why is the role open — growth, backfill, or a new venue?
3. How many stages are there, who is in each, and what is the timeline?
4. How does the 07:00–18:00 weekly rota actually work, and how often are evenings or weekends?
5. Is the London minimum three days fixed days, and which office?
6. What salary band did Aquis give you, and what sits around base — bonus, pension, benefits?
7. Is there a technical test or case, or is the next round a conversation?
8. Which platforms does this seat cover first — Aquis Markets, client exchanges, AQSE — or all of them?
9. How large is the Exchange Operations team?
10. When do they want someone to start? I’m available immediately, with full UK right to work and no notice period.

If they ask your number, stay on £85,000–£95,000 for this London shift role and ask what band they are working to.

## Save for Jack

For Jack, the hiring manager (Head of Second Line Operations), not the recruiter.

"What does the onboarding and ramp-up period look like for someone coming into the Exchange Operations team?"

"What are the primary platforms or trading venues the team is focusing on scaling up over the next 6 months?"
