# Job spec — Operations Analyst

## Presence

- Aquis maintains a presence in both the UK and EU (primarily Paris).
- Hybrid: mixture of office and WFH.
- London minimum 3 days in the office.

## Reports to

Head of Second Line Operations

## Job overview

- Primary responsibilities are maintaining and running the Aquis and Aquis client exchange platforms.
- Aquis is growing; the list of exchanges the Exchange Operations Team supports may grow.
- Weekly shift rota 07:00–18:00.
- There may also be some evening and weekend work.

## Responsibilities

- Day-to-day management of the exchange platforms, with other operations analysts
- 1st line support to connected clients
- Work with Compliance, Surveillance, Infrastructure and Sales so live operations of all platforms are appropriately monitored
- Support build-out and configuring of new exchange platforms
- Lead role in technology client support: platform delivery management, support, and knowledge transfer
- Raise and deploy changes for day-to-day running of multiple exchange platforms
- Compile outage reports for production outages once rectified
- Subject matter expert of the exchange platforms from a technological perspective
- Take part in release review meetings prior to upgrades
- Actively raise and pursue improvements that benefit the team and the wider Aquis ecosystem

## Essential experience

- Degree (or equivalent) and/or 5 years
- Extensive Unix/Linux
- Advanced SQL and relational databases
- Scripting (bash/shell, python or similar)
- Microsoft Excel and Office
- FIX Protocol
- GIT or similar
- Explain technical concepts simply to a non-technical audience
- Supporting production applications and end-to-end systems
- Network support and troubleshooting
- Exposure to UK and EU equity markets

## About Aquis

Europe’s challenger exchange: primary listings and secondary trading, plus global licensing of proprietary technology. Part of SIX (Swiss and Spanish financial infrastructure). [www.aquis.eu](https://www.aquis.eu)

### Four divisions

- **Aquis Markets** — lit and dark books, 16 European markets. Lit books use subscription pricing on message traffic, not a percentage of trade value.
- **Aquis Technologies** — matching engine and trade surveillance, licensed.
- **AQSE** — Recognised Investment Exchange. Growth Market Access for earlier-stage companies and Apex for larger businesses.
- **Aquis Data** — sells data from Aquis Markets and AQSE to non-members.

### Regulation

Authorised by UK FCA and France’s ACPR and AMF for MTF businesses in UK & Switzerland and EU27 respectively.
