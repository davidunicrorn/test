# 60-Second Background Pitch

## Open the call

"Hi Jack, thanks for making time today. Before we get into my background, I need to flag a version error on the CV I sent yesterday. It still listed Crypto.com as my current role. My time there wrapped up last April, after a company-wide restructuring. I’ve just sent the updated CV with the correct dates. Sorry for the confusion.

That aside, a quick summary of the background: I’ve spent over 7 years in technical support and exchange operations. Most recently, five years at Crypto.com on L2/L3 production support, triaging client escalations, coordinating bug fixes with engineering, and running the work in Jira and GitHub. Before that, at TS Imagine, I supported multi-asset trading and FIX order flow for institutional clients."

## If they ask nothing else

Say the correction once, send the file, and don’t return to it unless they ask.

## Pitch on its own

If the CV point is already done:

"Hi Jack, to give you a quick summary: I’ve spent over 7 years in technical support and exchange operations. Most recently, I spent five years at Crypto.com focused on L2/L3 production support, triaging client escalations, coordinating bug resolutions with engineering, and managing operational workflows in Jira and GitHub. Prior to that, at TS Imagine, I supported multi-asset trading and handled FIX protocol order flows for institutional clients."
